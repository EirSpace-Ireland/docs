# UAS Team

The UAS Team designs, builds, and tests uncrewed aircraft systems with a focus on rapid iteration and flight-ready documentation.

## Key focus

- Airframe design and integration
- Propulsion and power sizing
- Avionics, controls, and autonomy
- Flight test planning and operations
